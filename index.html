Suits Video Worker

A private-use video processing tool for its operator. It renders video clips on the operator's VPS and uploads them to a designated Google Drive folder.

This is not a public sign-up service. This website is informational and has no sign-in form.

Google Drive Access

The app requests only the drive.file permission: access to files it creates or files explicitly selected for it. It does not request access to all Drive files, Gmail, or contacts.
