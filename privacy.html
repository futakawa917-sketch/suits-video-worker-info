Suits Video Worker

Privacy Policy

Suits Video Worker is a private-use video processing tool operated for its owner's video workflow. This policy describes its handling of Google user data.

Access and Purpose

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits access to files created by the app or explicitly selected for it, including their viewing, creation, editing and deletion. It does not request access to all Drive files, Gmail, or contacts.

File names, IDs, parent folder IDs, sizes and checksums are used to route uploads, verify file integrity and prevent duplicate deliveries. Video contents are uploaded to Google Drive as instructed by the operator.

Storage and Service Providers

OAuth credentials are stored in a restricted file on the operator's ConoHa VPS. Upload records and temporary media are stored there for processing and troubleshooting. Credentials are not published in source repositories or logs.

Data is processed using Google Drive and the operator's ConoHa VPS. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Uploaded files follow the sharing permissions of the selected Drive folder. The app does not make uploaded files public on its own.

Retention and Control

The operator retains uploaded videos in Drive until they choose to remove them. Processing records and temporary media remain on the VPS until removed by the operator or the configured workflow; there is no fixed retention period.

The operator can revoke the app through Google Account third-party access settings and remove its credentials, temporary files and records from the VPS. Revoking access does not delete files already uploaded to Drive. The operator can manage those files in Drive.

Google API Policy

The app's use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the operator at snshack.co@gmail.com.
